Observing the world, one frame at a time.

I'm a photographer and visual storyteller based in Assam, drawn to quiet moments, changing light and the mood a place carries long after you leave it.

I look for atmosphere before subject: how shadow falls, how weather softens a landscape, how a small cultural detail can hold a whole story. Each frame is an attempt to make that feeling visible.
